# Research: Implicit scheduling rules mined from current & recent schedules

Resolution of [ScheduleLib-6xo.11](beads) — Research: Mine current schedules for implicit rules. Report produced by a research subagent, 2026-09-05. Worktree `feat/schedule-engine-wayfinder` @ f513629. Deliverable purpose: a **ranked list of candidate rules** (evidence + confidence + taxonomy mapping + suggested default weight) to seed the builder's default constraint set. This does **not** decide which rules to adopt — the user approves that in the constraint-model grilling.

## 1. Method & corpus

Two complementary datasets were mined with throwaway Python (`/tmp`, python-docx + openpyxl; nothing in the repo was modified):

1. **Parsed fixtures (official parser output, primary evidence).** `src/ScheduleLib/Tests/ScheduleFromDoc/schedule_Default.json` = 2024-25 S2 (1 022 `RegularLessons` over Periods 0/1 = the 2 docx snapshots, + Period −1 = 50 master-group lessons from `2024_sem2/zi/master.docx`; 33 groups, 82 teachers) and `src/ScheduleLib/Tests/ScheduleFromDoc/schedule_New.json` = 2025-26 S1 (1 559 `RegularLessons` over 3 Periods = 501/529/529, + 618 `OneTimeLessons`; 43 groups of which 13 FR, 91 teachers). `src/Tests/data/schedule_2025_1.json` is byte-identical to `schedule_New.json` (md5 `a08c1c7f…`), so it adds no third dataset. Fixture lessons carry typed `LessonType`, `Parity`, `TimeSlot`, `Period`, normalized `Room` — the same model the engine will seed from (see parsing-pipeline-recon.md §2).
2. **Raw docs (coverage the fixtures lack: 2024-25 S1, 2025-26 S2, 2026-27 S1).** All 24 docx + 4 xlsx under `src/Features/Integration/data/` were re-parsed with a parser mirroring `WordScheduleParser` semantics: time from the `I / 8:00-9:30` time column (`WordScheduleParser.cs:745-760`), in-cell leading `HH:MM` as a time override (`LessonParser.cs:525-535`, `ParsingStep.TimeOverride`), `w:vMerge continue` cells skipped, consecutive same-(day,time) rows treated as odd/even-row pairs (`(imp)`/`(par)` markers disambiguate; identical cells merge into one EveryWeek lesson — this is how the hand-written format encodes parity). That yields per-semester "group-cell entries" (one record per group column, so a merged 3-group curs counts 3× — the student-facing view; fixture uniques are used where per-lesson counts matter): 352 (2024s1, root `Orar_An_II Lic.docx`), 807 (2024s2, snapshot `19.03.25`), 839 (2025s1, `05.11.25`), 829 (2025s2, `13.02.26`), 884 (2026s1, `01.09.26`). Excel: `2025_sem2/zi/master.xlsx` (21 typed entries, master evening) and the FR session workbooks `2025_sem1/fr/1.xlsx` (556), `2025_sem2/fr/1.xlsx` (748), `2025_sem2/fr/2.xlsx` (401).

Slot indices below are 0-based, matching the fixture JSON and `DefaultLessonTimeConfig`: **s0 08:00–09:30, s1 09:45–11:15, s2 11:30–13:00, s3 13:15–14:45, s4 15:00–16:30, s5 16:45–18:15, s6 18:30–20:00** (`src/ScheduleLib/ScheduleLib.Core/Model/ImmutableModels/TimeSlot.cs:131-148`). "Morning" = s0–s2; "late" = s5–s6. Day-load and "last of day" are computed **per (group, day, parity-week)**: EveryWeek lessons occupy both weeks, Odd/Even only their week. Multi-snapshot semesters use the **latest snapshot only** (earlier snapshots double-count moved lessons). Where fixture and raw disagree slightly, fixture numbers win (official parse); raw is corroboration across the years fixtures don't cover. Room-kind classes are taken from room-inventory.md §3 (not re-derived).

## 2. Ranked candidate rules

Weight = suggested default **preference weight on the relative 1–10 scale** (10 = strongest); Requirements are hard, weight n/a.

### R1 (Requirement, n/a) — A group is never double-booked; apparent overlaps are expressed splits, not accidents

Evidence: in both fixtures (latest snapshot), same-(group, day, slot, week) multi-lesson slots are almost always *labelled* splits: parallel subgroup sections (SubGroup I/II, `SubGroup` field) 46/303 slot-weeks in 2024s2 (`schedule_Default.json` P1) and 38/314 in 2025s1 (`schedule_New.json` P2); the residual "no-split" overlaps are 9 per semester and inspection shows they are all parallel **alternatives/electives or write artifacts**: e.g. IA2301 Sat s1 `Containerizare (lab)` spec=CV vs `React (lab)` sub=WR1/WR2 (three rooms, one slot — students pick one), and DJ2402 Sat s0-s3 `HTML` + `CSS și JavaScript` in the same room 122/4 (two courses stacked in one source cell, `2024_sem2/zi/19.03.25/Orar_An_II Lic.docx`). Confidence: high (structural, both semesters). Implication: the hard constraint is real but the model must be able to *express* co-timed subgroup sections (multi-room at one slot) and co-timed elective alternatives (the bloc gap, group-structure-2026-2027.md §4 gap 2; the existing overlap allowlist `src/ScheduleDefaults/Config.cs:104-172` is the hand-maintained copy of this fact).

### R2 (Requirement, n/a) — Teacher no-overlap holds in intent; parsed data carries 11–25 same-slot pairs per semester that are artifacts

Evidence: same-(teacher, day, slot, week) pairs in latest snapshots: 11 (2024s2), 25 (2025s1). Classification: 2025s1 → 23 of 25 are *same course + same room* (the same physical section written once per group column; e.g. `Rețele de calculatoare` I2401 + IA2402 both 145a/4 Thu s4), 2 are different-course same-name pairs (`V.Guțu` teaching both `Matematica` (213a/4, 415/4) and `Designul narativ p/u jocuri` (401/4) Mon s1–s2 — raw cells in `2025_sem1/zi/05.11.25/Orar_An_I Lic.docx` confirm the identical short name; almost certainly two people sharing short initials, since the model keys teachers by name). 2024s2 → 4 same-course+same-room, 3 same-course-different-room (parallel sections: `Limbaje formale` 251/4 + 219/4a Thu s5; `Spring Framework` 404/4 + 218/4 Wed s1), 4 stacked-cell artifacts (T.Paša HTML+CSS), 1 real-looking two-course overlap (`D.Pugaci` Mon s5, 350/4 vs 423/4). Confidence: the *rule* is hard; the *data* needs a pre-merge step. Implication: teacher no-overlap is safe as a Requirement, but seeded input must merge same-(course, room, slot) rows first and tolerate ~2–7 genuine-looking same-name conflicts per semester (or resolve teacher identity via the website full-name enrichment, ScheduleLoader.cs:145-178).

### R3 (Requirement + Preference, hard + w≈6) — Group day-load: ≤5 distinct slots is the norm, 6 is the observed ceiling — confirms the decided "6 hard / 5 soft"

Evidence (parity-aware, latest snapshots): 2024s2 group-day-week load dist {1:10, 2:63, 3:87, 4:113, 5:30} — 100% ≤5 (303/303); 2025s1 {1:6, 2:56, 3:74, 4:122, 5:55, 6:1} — 99.7% ≤5 (313/314, the single 6 is IA2302 Thursday odd-week, slots 1–6). Raw corroboration across 5 semesters: mode 3–4, max 5–6 (≤5 in 98.7–100% of group-days). Lesson-count load (counting parallel subgroup sections separately) adds at most +2 to a day. Confidence: high (n≈617 group-day-weeks in fixtures, ~1 330 raw). The already-decided default (student day-load 6 hard / 5 soft) fits the data with headroom: hard 6 binds only on ~1 observed day per semester; note a student-day counted in *lessons* (splits separate) can exceed a 6-slot count — the engine must define which unit the cap applies to (grilling call).

### R4 (Preference, w≈8) — Group days are gapless; an internal free slot is rare

Evidence: internal gaps (free slots between first and last lesson) = 0 for 285/303 (94.1%) group-day-weeks in 2024s2 and 287/314 (91.4%) in 2025s1 (fixtures); raw 2024s1/2025s2/2026s1: 81%, 88%, 90% gapless. 1-gap days: 5–8%; ≥2 gaps: ≤2%. The gaps that do exist cluster on elective-heavy afternoons. Confidence: high, consistent all 5 semesters. This is the strongest *positive* soft pattern in the corpus; suggest seeding "compact group day" at weight 8.

### R5 (structural Requirement; day preferences w≈6–7) — PE: no room, concentrated on Tue/Thu, never in the late slots — but *not* "last of the day"

Evidence, pooled over 5 semesters (raw PE entries 85 + fixture PE records 78):

- **Room**: PE carries no room in 100% of cases (`Room: null` in fixtures — 32/32 PE records in `schedule_Default.json`, 46/46 in `schedule_New.json`; 75/75 raw entries across 2024s1–2026s1, e.g. `Educ. fizică (imp)` with no room, `2026_sem1/zi/01.09.26/Orar_An_I Lic.docx` Marţi I row). Off-site halls; the model must keep null-room lessons outside the room axis (room-inventory.md §4 already says this).
- **Days**: Tue 40/85 (47%), Thu 40/85 (47%), Fri 5/85 (6%) — raw per semester: 2024s1 Thu 10/10; 2024s2 Tue 11 + Thu 9; 2025s1 Tue 14 + Thu 10 + Fri 4; 2025s2 Tue 10 + Thu 2 + Fri 1; 2026s1 Thu 9 + Tue 5.
- **Slots** (raw): s0 16 / s1 16 / s2 14 / s3 23 / s4 16 / **s5 0 / s6 0** — **PE never occupies s5–s6 in any semester**, and in the last two years rarely s0–s1 either (2025s2: all ≤s3; 2026s1: all ≤s3).
- **Last-of-day** (per group-week, parity-correct): 2024s1 20%, 2024s2 58–60%, 2025s1 35–36%, 2025s2 14%, 2026s1 43% (fixtures: 58% / 35%). Failures are spread: PE runs *first* for An I in 2025s1 (e.g. `M2501/I2501` PE Tue s0 with lessons s1–s4 after; `schedule_New.json` P2).

Confidence: day/slot confinement high (n≈107 PE incidences: 85 raw + 22 fixture unique-lesson group incidences); "PE last" **rejected as a regularity** (§3 N2). Mapping: keep null-room as structural requirement; "PE on Tue/Thu" and "PE not in s5–s6" as preferences w≈6–7; the pre-registered "PE scheduled last (soft)" default is **not supported** by the hand-written schedules and should be re-weighed by the user (see §5).

### R6 (modeling feature, confirmed) — Odd/even-week splits are real and lab-heavy

Evidence: 17–24% of raw group-entries carry explicit `(imp)`/`(par)` markers (2024s1 22%, 2024s2 22%, 2025s1 22%, 2025s2 17%, 2026s1 24%); in fixtures 26% of 2025s1 records are Odd/Even (400/1 559) and parity-pairs (same course+groups+slot, opposite parity) = 17 (2025s1) / 23 (2024s2). By lesson kind (raw): **Lab 26–35%** split, Seminar 8–30%, Unspecified 18–59%, **Curs only 3–11%**. Top split courses 2025s1: `Limba straina`, `IA pentru Jocuri`, `Baze de date`, `Matematica`, `Grafică și animație 2D` (`schedule_New.json`); PE splits half the time (2024s2 16/16 lessons split, 2025s1 7/16). The mechanism is documented operationally by `data/Paritate.docx` ("PARITATEA SĂPTĂMÂNILOR, Semestrul II 2024-2025": week-number → Pară/Impară table) and by `master.xlsx` (4 imp / 2 par markers). Confidence: high. Implication: parity is a confirmed modeling feature (already decided); its *cost* shows up mostly for lab-room demand — a split lab pair occupies two rooms alternately, halving effective lab need for that course.

### R7 (modeling note; mode-scoped) — The zi weekly grid ends at 20:00 and underuses both day edges; FR/master are different tracks

Evidence: s6 (18:30) holds 0–3% of zi group-entries per semester (fixtures: 7/486 unique lessons in 2024s2 P1, 16/529 in 2025s1 P2; raw 2026s1: 79 s5+s6 entries of 884 = 9%, of which 59 are An III — `Psihologie`, `Realitate virtuală și augmentată`, `SAWM`, `Design soft`). First slot = s0 in only 43–49% of group-days, s0–s1 in 70–72%, s3+ in 16% (fixtures; raw agrees 32–48% s0). FR groups have **zero** weekly-grid lessons in `schedule_New.json` — their 618 lessons are `OneTimeLessons` (dated: Lab 322 / Prelegere 211 / Seminar 85, spread Mon–Sun incl. Sat 107 / Sun 119), matching the FR session workbooks whose columns are dated days with slots 13:15–20:00 (`2025_sem1/fr/1.xlsx` sheet `sem I`). Master 2025s2 runs evening-only (slots labeled I 15:00–16:30 … III 18:30–20:00, `2025_sem2/zi/master.xlsx`); the 2024s2 master.docx lessons are mostly online (`Room: null` in 42/50 Period −1 lessons, `schedule_Default.json`). Confidence: high. Implication: hard slot-window = the 7 configured slots; late slots are a real but elective/upper-year resource; FR/master should not be seeded onto the weekly grid at all (recon §5.2 already flags OneTime as a separate seeding category — this confirms it empirically).

### R8 (Preference, w≈3–4; keep Saturday schedulable) — Saturday is a genuine elective day for upper years

Evidence: Saturday group-day-weeks exist in every semester (2024s2: 36/303; 2025s1: 52/314); groups using all 6 days: 13/28 (2024s2) and 7/30 (2025s1) (days-used/week dist: 2024s2 {5:14, 6:13, 2:1}, 2025s1 {5:23, 6:7}); Saturday composition is elective-heavy: 2024s2 Saturday types Lab 26 / Curs 21 / Unspecified 4 / Seminar 2 with courses `Containerizare și virtualizare`, `Spring Framework`, `React`, `HTML`, `CSS și JavaScript`, `Managementul proiectelor` (`schedule_Default.json` P1); 2026s1 raw: An III 33 of 53 (`Securitatea aplicațiilor web și mobile`, `Design audio și efecte vizuale`, `Proiect.JV`, `GA2D`). An I zi rarely has core Saturday lessons (2025s1: only DJ2502, the dual group). Confidence: medium-high (composition consistent; the "elective" classification is an inference from course names). Implication: no hard weekday limit; a mild penalty on Saturday for core/An I would match practice, but optionals must remain free to land there.

### R9 (Preference, w≈2–3; extends room-inventory §4 with the time axis) — Big-curs rooms are a morning/midday resource; labs run all day into the evening

Evidence (per-snapshot, zi lessons with rooms): big lecture rooms 113/4 + 213a/4 + 404/4 hold 71 lessons in 2025s1 P2, of which 3 (4%) at s5–s6; 2024s2 (deduped across the two snapshots): 77 lessons, 8 (10%) at s5–s6. Lab-kind rooms (room-inventory §3.1 set): 305 lessons in 2025s1 P2, 42 (14%) at s5–s6, 27 at s5 alone; 2024s2: 319 lessons, 21 (7%) at s5–s6 — the evening lab load roughly doubles into 2025-26 (s5 lab lessons 15 → 27). Room-kind × slot, 2025s1 P2: lab rooms {0:35, 1:53, 2:65, 3:70, 4:40, 5:27, 6:15}, big-curs {0:9, 1:13, 2:21, 3:14, 4:11, 5:2, 6:1}, other rooms {0:17, 1:30, 2:34, 3:38, 4:16, 5:2, 6:0}, no-room {0:3, 1:2, 2:3, 3:3, 4:5}. Confidence: high (same shape both fixtures + raw 2026s1: s5–s6 users are An III labs/electives). Implication: room-kind feasibility needs lab capacity through s5–s6 (upper years), while curs-room demand is concentrated s0–s3 — relevant when the engine shares the room pool across years. Also: labs are the scarce evening resource; curs rooms sit empty late.

### R10 (Preference, w≈3) — A (course, group, lesson-kind) pair tends to keep one room

Evidence: (course, group, type) triples with ≥2 lessons landing in a single room: 113/170 = 66% (2024s2), 98/163 = 60% (2025s1) — much stronger than course-level stability (§3 N3) because the same course runs sections for different groups in different rooms. Confidence: medium (denominator includes legit multi-room splits: subgroup sections co-timed in different labs). Implication: "keep the room a group is used to for a course" is a real but soft habit; room stability across semester snapshots is already established in room-inventory.md §4.

### R11 (Preference, w≈3; likely a byproduct) — Teacher days are compact too

Evidence: teacher-day internal gaps = 0 for 310/346 (90%) teacher-day-weeks in 2024s2 and 373/393 (95%) in 2025s1 (fixtures, parity-aware). Teachers with ≥6 weekly lessons teach on 3.28 days mean (median 3) in 2024s2 / 3.69 (median 4) in 2025s1; busiest-day share of their load averages 0.48 / 0.43. Many teachers are effectively part-time: 18 of 75 (2024s2) / 33 of 84 (2025s1) teach on exactly 1 day-week. Confidence: high counts, but causality unclear — group-day compactness (R4) mechanically compresses teacher days. Implication: a low-weight "compact teacher day" preference mirrors reality without dominating; teacher *day*-bunching beyond this is not a habit (§3 N6).

### R12 (Preference, w≈3) — Days are front-loaded (start 08:00–09:45 in ~70% of group-days)

Evidence: first-occupied-slot dist (fixtures, parity-aware): 2024s2 {0:147, 1:72, 2:36, 3:24, 4:19, 5:5} → s0 49%, s0–s1 72%, s3+ 16%; 2025s1 → s0 43%, s0–s1 70%, s3+ 16%. Days end at s3–s4 in 64–66% of group-days; s5–s6 ends = 17–20% (upper-year electives). Raw agrees (s0 start 32–48%). Confidence: high counts; the effect size is partly mechanical (a 4–5 lesson gapless day in a 7-slot grid naturally starts early), so weight low.

## 3. Tested and NOT confirmed (negative results)

- **N1 — "Curs in the morning, labs in the afternoon"**: rejected. Morning share of group-entries: Curs 55–65% across 5 semesters, Lab 50–72%, Seminar 23–67% (2024s1 seminars are 77% *afternoon*). Distributions overlap heavily; labs are scheduled all day every year (raw slot dists, §2 R9 table). The only kind-related time facts that hold are R7/R9 (late slots = An-III electives in lab rooms; big-curs rooms empty after 15:00).
- **N2 — "PE is scheduled last in the day"**: rejected as an observed regularity. Correctly computed (parity-aware, per group-week) PE-last = 20% / 58–60% / 35–36% / 14% / 43% over 2024s1…2026s1. The naive higher figure (95%) is an artifact of ignoring EveryWeek lessons after a parity-split PE lesson. The true invariant is slot ≤ s4 + Tue/Thu (R5). The pre-decided soft default needs the user's re-weighing (§5).
- **N3 — "Same course always on the same day / same slot"**: rejected. Courses(type) with ≥2 lessons on a single day: 19/109 (17%) in 2024s2, 18/84 (21%) in 2025s1; single slot: 24/109 (22%) and 8/84 (10%) (`schedule_{Default,New}.json` unique lessons). Multi-section courses spread deliberately.
- **N4 — "A lunch break is visible in the grid"**: rejected. There is no lunch slot (7 contiguous 90-min slots, `TimeSlot.cs:131-148`); mid-day sandwich holes (s3 free between s0–s2 and s4–s6) occur in 13/327 (2024s2) and 28/289 (2025s1) group-day snapshots — no special lunch structure beyond R4's generic gaplessness.
- **N5 — "An I avoids 08:00" (or any year-level morning avoidance)**: rejected. An I group-prefix first-slot s0 counts are the *largest* of all prefixes (2024s2: IA s0 58/109 group-days, M s0 18/30; 2025s1: IA s0 57/130). An III is the group that drifts late (evening electives, R7/R9).
- **N6 — "Teachers bunch their week onto few consecutive days"**: not confirmed beyond R11's within-day compactness. Busiest-day share 0.43–0.48 mean with a broad distribution; days/week tracks load, not a bunching habit. Only 15–19 of 36–43 heavy teachers put ≥50% of their load on one day.
- **N7 — "FR/master follow the weekly zi grid"**: rejected — FR lessons are dated `OneTimeLesson`s (incl. weekends, 618 in `schedule_New.json`), FR/master workbooks schedule 13:15–20:00 session slots (`2025_sem1/fr/1.xlsx`, `2025_sem2/zi/master.xlsx`); master 2024s2 was mostly online (42/50 roomless).
- **N8 — "Parity marks indicate different rooms across weeks"**: not specifically tested per-room, but no evidence of room alternation surfaced; parity pairs share the slot and typically swap subgroup sections in the *same* room (e.g. CNMO I/II alternation on IA2301 Tue s3, both 412/c, `schedule_Default.json` P1). Treat parity as occupancy-split only (matches `Parity.IsMatch`, Model/ImmutableModels/Parity.cs).

## 4. Data-quality findings that constrain seeding (cross-cutting)

1. **Same-course + same-room + same-slot rows written per group column** (23/25 of 2025s1 teacher pairs) must merge before hard constraints evaluate; the parser merges only fully identical rows (`AddOrMergeLesson`, WordScheduleParser.cs:874-1007).
2. **Short-name teacher collisions** (`V.Guțu` ×2 people; `M.Butnaru` vs `N.Butnari` both visible in 2024s2 An III cells) — teacher identity needs the website full-name enrichment before "no-overlap" is enforced.
3. **Stacked cells with explicit times** (`8:00 Pedagogie (curs) ⏎ 13:15 Ped. (sem)` inside one 8:00-labeled row) are frequent in 2026 docs; raw counts that ignore them shift slot distributions by ~10% — engine seed data must come from the official parser (fixtures), not naive table reads.
4. **Parity-marker default**: cells identical across the odd/even row-pair carry no marker and mean EveryWeek; differing cells always carry `(imp)`/`(par)`. No differing-unmarked cells were observed in 2026 snapshots (checked `Orar_An_{I,II} Lic.docx` 01.09.26).
5. `EDP` (6 records, room 214/4, `schedule_Default.json`) is *not* PE despite the similar abbreviation; PE detection by name `fizic` is exact here.

## 5. Seeding implications (pre-registration candidates vs user's call)

**Looks safe to pre-register in the builder defaults** (consistent across all 5 semesters, structural, or already decided and confirmed):

- Requirement: group no-overlap + teacher no-overlap (R1, R2) — with the pre-merge + teacher-identity cleanups of §4.
- Requirement: hard day-load cap 6 (R3) — confirms the decided default with headroom; define the unit (slots vs lessons) first.
- Structural: 7-slot day window s0–s6 (08:00–20:00); null-room lessons outside the room axis (R5, R7); parity as occupancy split (R6); co-timed subgroup sections need multi-room assignment (R1).
- Preference defaults: gapless group day w≈8 (R4); PE not in s5–s6 w≈7 and PE on Tue/Thu w≈6 (R5); day-load ≤5 soft w≈6 (R3); Saturday-free-for-An-I-core w≈3–4 (R8); curs-room mornings w≈2–3 / lab-evening feasibility (R9); per-(course,group) room stability w≈3 (R10); compact teacher day w≈3 (R11); front-loaded days w≈3 (R12).

**Needs the user's call in the constraint-model grilling**:

- **PE-last (pre-decided soft default)**: the schedules contradict it (14–60% adherence). Keep at low weight (≤2), replace with the observed Tue/Thu + slot≤s4 pattern, or drop — user decides.
- **Day-load cap unit**: distinct slots vs lesson count (subgroup splits count?) — decided default says "student day-load 6/5"; data supports 6 *slots* hard with essentially zero violations, and 5 soft.
- Whether "electives tolerate Saturday/late slots" should be an explicit relaxation of the gap/load preferences for optional courses rather than a global penalty (R8, R7).
- Whether teacher compactness (R11) is worth a separate term at all, given it is likely a byproduct of group compactness.
